## Corrected story differences

The AppDialog warning fixture had changed from the original `text-red-500` on
`bg-red-50/60` to destructive tokens. Its long-content fixture had also changed
slate text/background to muted tokens. Both fixtures were restored before final
registration; the incorrect destructive pair is not accepted as inherited debt.

The gray UnitList mode-switch sample belongs to the story wrapper, not the
production toolbar. `src/stories/features/UnitList.stories.tsx:252-275` already
uses `text-gray-500 hover:text-gray-700` on `bg-gray-100`. It is inherited.

The final element-by-element register is `.storybook/contrast-baseline.json`.
The table records original style sources; translucent and image-backed colors
are measured with fixed story fixtures, rather than assumed to be universal
background values.
